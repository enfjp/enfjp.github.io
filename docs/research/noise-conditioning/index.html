Is Noise Conditioning Necessary? A Unified Theory of Unconditional Graph Diffusion Models

Jipeng Li, Yanning Shen

NeurIPS 2025Spotlight

IN ONE QUESTION

Can a denoiser infer the noise level from a corrupted graph, rather than receiving a separate noise-level input?

The question

Graph diffusion models typically receive an explicit noise-level input. The paper asks when a corrupted graph already contains information about the noise level, and when a denoiser can use that information without a separate conditioning signal.

The approach

The analysis starts with Bernoulli edge-flip corruption and extends to settings with coupled structure and attribute noise. Experiments compare conditioned and unconditioned models in GDSS and DiGress on synthetic and real-world graph datasets.

What we found

Under the evaluated settings, unconditioned graph diffusion models achieve comparable or better generation performance with fewer parameters and lower computation time. The claim is specific to the analyzed assumptions and experimental settings, not a claim that every diffusion model can discard noise conditioning.
