I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of California, Irvine, advised by Yanning Shen. My research interests include graph learning, generative models, and reliable decision-making.

My recent work studies when graph diffusion models can infer noise levels without explicit conditioning, and how graph invariants can support ownership verification for graph neural networks.

Before joining UCI, I completed graduate studies at UC Davis and Tsinghua University, following my undergraduate education at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC).

Outside research, I photograph landscapes, wildlife, and the places I pass through.
